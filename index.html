How to Play

  • Shapes slide in from any side, then slow down
  • Press, drag and let go of it to draw a straight line across a shape.
  • Your line must start and end outside the shape
  • Cut as close 50 / 50 as you can. 45 / 55 or better scores +1
  • A worse cut, or a shape that gets away, costs 1 point
  • Reach the goal before the 60 seconds run out to clear the level.
  • Higher levels bring smaller, faster and stranger shapes

About

Shape Splitter is a game about splitting things fairly. Every shape has to be cut into 2 equal halves with one straight line
